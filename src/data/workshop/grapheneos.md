GrapheneOS is a security and privacy focused mobile operating system with Android app compatibility. It's a non-profit open source project.

### Key Features:
- **Sandbox:** Improved sandboxing for apps.
- **Privacy:** Designed to prevent tracking and data collection.
- **Security:** Hardened kernel and hardened allocator.
- **Compatibility:** Runs most Android apps without Google Play Services.
